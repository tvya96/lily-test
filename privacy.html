Privacy Notice

Last updated: 25 August 2026

1. About this notice

This privacy notice explains how Lily & Light Therapy Services may handle information you provide when you use this website or contact the service.

2. Information you may provide

Depending on how you contact us, this may include your name, email address, phone number, appointment preferences and the contents of your enquiry.

Please avoid submitting highly sensitive health information through a general website enquiry form unless specifically requested through an appropriate secure channel.

3. How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, arrange appointments, communicate with you about services, and meet applicable professional, legal or administrative obligations.

4. Confidentiality and therapy records

Website enquiries and therapeutic records may be handled differently. Confidentiality of therapy is subject to applicable professional, legal and safeguarding requirements. Your therapist can explain the relevant arrangements before or during your first session.

5. Third-party services

This website may use third-party services such as GitHub Pages for hosting and Google Fonts for typography. Those services may process technical information according to their own policies.

6. Cookies and analytics

The starter version of this website does not intentionally use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. If analytics, booking tools or other services are added later, this notice should be updated accordingly.

7. Data retention

Information may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, professional and record-keeping requirements.

8. Your rights

Your privacy rights depend on the laws that apply to you and the location in which the service operates. You may contact Lily & Light Therapy Services to ask about information held about you or to raise a privacy concern.
