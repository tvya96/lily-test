QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

If you have a question that isn't answered here, you're welcome to get in touch.

What happens in the first session?

The first session is an opportunity to talk about what has brought you to therapy, what you hope to work on and what support may be appropriate. You can ask questions too.

Is therapy confidential?

Therapy is generally confidential, subject to applicable professional, legal and safeguarding requirements. Your therapist can explain the relevant boundaries of confidentiality during your first session.

How long is a session?

Session length and frequency can vary. Please contact Lily & Light Therapy Services for the current session duration and scheduling options.

Can I have online sessions?

Availability of online or in-person sessions depends on the service offered and your location. Please enquire for current options.

What if I'm not sure whether therapy is right for me?

That's okay. An initial enquiry can simply be a conversation about what you're looking for and whether the service may be a suitable fit.
